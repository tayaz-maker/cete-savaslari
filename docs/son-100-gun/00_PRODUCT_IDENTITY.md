# Son 100 Gün — Product Identity

## Player fantasy
You have exactly 100 days. Every meaningful action consumes irreplaceable time. This is not a life sim shortened to 100 turns — it is built around scarcity as the core tension from day one.

## What it is NOT
- **Not** TC SIM shortened to 100 turns — TC SIM's weekly-decision-cap dashboard is not reused wholesale; the whole UI and pacing is redesigned around visible, shrinking time.
- **Not** a countdown skin on an otherwise normal life sim.
- **Not** a survival roguelike, unless a specific scenario explicitly calls for it (none do in V1).

## Signature mechanic: Time Scarcity / Opportunity Cost
The player cannot do everything. Every day's limited action capacity forces a real trade-off between money, health, relationships, and obligations — and the trade-off must be visible *before* the player commits, not just felt afterward.

## Scenario-based structure
V1 ships as a scenario picker, not one fixed story:
- Borçları toparla
- Aileyle barış
- İşini yeniden kur
- Hayatını düzene sok
- Büyük bir hedefe hazırlan

Avoid a medical terminal-illness framing by default — it's the obvious "100 days left" trope and forecloses tone options; may be added later as one scenario among several, never the default framing.

## Base engine
Primary reuse: TC SIM (time/event/save engine). Secondary reuse: Hanedan's compressed, irreversible pacing sensibility (a wrong week matters, there's no long runway to recover).

## Progression
No XP. Progress = state transformation and resolved scenario goals, tracked across multiple outcome dimensions (finances, relationships, body, unresolved obligations, scenario objective) — no single "perfect" ending.

## Ending
Day 100 always ends the run. The ending report scores the outcome dimensions independently; there is no unified win/lose state.
