# Son 100 Gün — Sprint 1 Vertical Slice Brief

Must prove: **time scarcity + opportunity cost + final report**. A 30-day prototype, not the full 100 — extending to 100 days only happens after this slice's pacing is validated.

## Scope
- 30-day run length (scaled down from 100 for the vertical slice, per Section 8's explicit guidance)
- 3 of the 5 scenarios implemented (recommend: Borçları toparla, Aileyle barış, İşini yeniden kur — the three with the clearest obligation/relationship tension for proving the mechanic)
- A working obligation pool sized for 30 days (roughly 20–30 templates across the 3 scenarios)
- Milestone beats scaled to the 30-day length (e.g., proportional thresholds, or the same day-10/day-3/final structure re-anchored)
- A working final report scoring all outcome dimensions

## Acceptance for Sprint 1
- Player can complete a 30-day run in any of the 3 scenarios with at least 3 real obligation trade-offs made under visible time pressure.
- At least one obligation genuinely escalates (ignored past its window) and its consequence is visible in the final report.
- Final report shows independent scores across money/relationships/body/obligations/scenario-goal — no single pass/fail verdict.
- Save/load works across the 30-day span; slot isolation holds.
- No fatal console errors, no horizontal overflow at 320–430px.

## Explicitly out of scope for Sprint 1
Full 100-day length, the remaining 2 scenarios, full 60–100-template obligation library, milestone reflection prose beyond a placeholder-quality first draft.
